# Delivery assignment contract

An owner may personally operate the Cash Van without a second driver account or duplicate membership. Delivery work may be assigned to an active owner or driver membership. A sole active usable owner with no drivers is the default assignee. With multiple eligible memberships, an owner selects and may auditably reassign work.

Drivers are least-privileged, see only assigned operational projections, and cannot assign/reassign work. Internal delivery state is never customer tracking.
